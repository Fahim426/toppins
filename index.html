Our story

Baked in-house, made for your table

Toppins Bake and Cafe sits on the Guruvayur-Althara-Ponnani Road in Perumbadappu, near Puthanpally Central Madrassa. It is a warm community haven for families, friends and travellers seeking something freshly baked, a hot puff, or an indulgent cake.

We take pride in artisan techniques—from our slow-baked Lotus Biscoff cheesecake to our afternoon-fresh egg puffs and traditional Salankatiya. Our team is known locally for friendly, welcoming hospitality.

  • Baked in-houseSignature cakes, pastries and hot puffs from our own kitchen.
  • Made to orderBespoke celebration cakes crafted for your milestones.
